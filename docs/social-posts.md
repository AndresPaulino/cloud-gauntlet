## Posting notes

- **Links go in the reply or first comment.** Both platforms show posts with outside links in the body to fewer people. For one self-contained post, move the link into the body and accept a smaller reach.
- **Upload the .mp4 directly** on each platform instead of linking to it.
- **The music credit is required** by the track's licence, so keep that line somewhere in each thread.
- **The closing question on LinkedIn is a real one.** Replies tell you what to build next, and early comments help the post travel, so answer them in the first hour.
